Nimbus Notes Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026. This policy explains how Nimbus Labs Pvt. Ltd. handles personal data in the Nimbus Notes application.

Information We Collect

We collect your name, email address and account details when you create a Nimbus Notes account.

We collect device information such as your browser type, operating system, IP address and advertising identifier.

With your permission, we collect precise location data from your mobile device to attach a place to a note.

We record usage information, including the features you open and the time you spend in the application.

How We Use Information

We use your personal data to provide the note taking service, to authenticate you and to keep your notes synchronised across devices.

We use usage information and device identifiers to analyse product performance and to improve the application.

Sharing and Disclosure

We share personal data with third party analytics and advertising partners, who may use it to measure and personalise advertising.

We share account information with service providers that host our infrastructure and process payments on our behalf.

We may disclose information to public authorities where the law requires us to do so.

Data Retention

We retain your account information for 24 months after you close your account, and then delete it from our production systems.

We retain support correspondence and diagnostic records for as long as necessary to resolve disputes and meet our legal obligations.

Your Rights and Choices

You can access, correct or delete the personal data associated with your account from the application settings.

You can withdraw consent for location access at any time through your device settings.

You can opt out of personalised advertising from the privacy settings screen.

If you have a grievance about how we handle your data, you can write to privacy@nimbusnotes.example.

Children

Nimbus Notes is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

Security

We use encryption in transit and at rest, access controls and monitoring to protect personal information against unauthorised access.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified in the application before they take effect.

Contact Us

You can contact our data protection officer at privacy@nimbusnotes.example for any question about this policy.
